2First setup

The camera creates its own Wi-Fi network that you use to set it up.

Step 1. Turn the camera on

  1. Insert the memory card into the camera's slot (contacts facing the board).
  2. Connect the power. Wait about 10 seconds.

Step 2. Join the camera's network

  1. On your phone, open Settings → Wi-Fi.
  2. Find the network ESP32-Camera-XXXX (XXXX is four letters or digits) and connect. No password is needed.
  3. The setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1.
⚠️
Your phone may say “No internet connection” and offer to disconnect. Choose “Stay connected” — no internet is needed for this step.

Step 3. Follow the setup wizard

The wizard has four short steps. Switch the language with the УКР / ENG buttons at the top.

Step 4. Done!

The camera joins your home network and shows its address, for example http://192.168.1.57/.

  1. Tap Copy the address or take a photo of the screen.
  2. Switch your phone back to your home Wi-Fi.
  3. Open the address in the browser and log in with your new password.
  4. Bookmark the page or add it to the home screen — then the camera opens like an app.
✅
The ESP32-Camera-XXXX network disappears by itself after a few minutes — that's expected.
⚠️
“Couldn't connect”? Usually it's a wrong Wi-Fi password or a 5 GHz network. Tap Try again and check the password.
💡
An address like http://camera-xxxx.local/ also works on iPhone, Mac and most computers — no need to remember numbers.

Logging in

The camera asks for your user name and password each time you open it. Your browser can remember them.

After several wrong attempts, logging in is paused for a while — this protects against password guessing.

Forgot the password? See section 7. Troubleshooting.

Security

When the Security switch is on, the camera records video by itself as soon as it sees motion — including a few seconds from before the motion started.

While recording, ● Recording lights up at the top and Motion appears on the video.

💡
Further down the home screen: free space on the card, time of the last motion, Wi-Fi signal and the latest recordings.

4Recordings

All videos are stored on the memory card inside the camera — nothing goes to someone else's cloud.

  • Recordings are grouped by day: Today, Yesterday and so on.
  • Filter at the top: All, Motion or Manual.
  • Tap a recording to watch it.
  • The bin button next to a day deletes all recordings from that day.
♻️
When the card fills up, the oldest recordings are deleted automatically. You never need to clean up by hand.

Player

  • The slider seeks; the 1× button changes speed.
  • The ‹ › arrows go to the previous and next recording.
  • At the top: download the file to your phone or computer, delete, close.
  • Downloads are .mp4 videos that play on any phone or computer. At home the video is prepared while you wait, about as long as the clip itself, so keep the page open. If a Save button appears, tap it.

6Access from the internet

Watch the camera from work or on holiday — no router configuration needed.

  1. Open Settings → Remote access.
  2. Turn on Access over the internet.
  3. Enter the server address and registration code given to you by whoever set up the camera. (If the camera was prepared in advance, they're already filled in.)
  4. When ● Connected appears, copy Your link and open it on your phone. Log in with the same name and password.
🔒
The link is the key to your camera. Share it only with people you trust. If it ends up in the wrong hands, open Advanced and tap New link — the old one stops working immediately.

7Troubleshooting

Answers to the most common questions.

Forgot the password

🔁
  1. Press the RST button on the camera three times in a row, about a second apart.
  2. The camera opens the ESP32-Camera-XXXX network again.
  3. Connect to it — a page opens where you can set a new login, password and, if needed, Wi-Fi.

Recordings on the memory card are kept.

ProblemWhat to do
I can't see the ESP32-Camera-XXXX networkWait a minute after powering on. Check the power supply: a weak charger is the most common cause of restarts.
The setup page didn't open by itselfOpen a browser and go to 192.168.4.1.
The camera won't connect to Wi-FiCheck the password. The camera works with 2.4 GHz only — make sure that band is enabled on the router.
The camera's address won't openYour phone must be on the same home network as the camera. Try the numeric address http://192.168.x.x/.
Wi-Fi is gone, or the camera movedAfter a minute the camera opens ESP32-Camera-XXXX. Join it, log in with your usual password and, if needed, choose the new network in Settings → Wi-Fi. The internet link stays the same.
“Too many failed attempts”Several wrong passwords were entered. Wait the time shown and try again.
No recordings appearCheck that Security is on and the memory card is inserted (up to 32 GB, FAT32).
Too many unwanted recordingsLower the motion sensitivity and remove things that move in the wind from the view.
The picture is upside downSettings → Picture: turn on Flip and Mirror.
Dark or washed-out pictureSettings → Picture: brightness and contrast.
